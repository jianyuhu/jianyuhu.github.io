## Research interests

Geometry, dynamical systems, statistics, and machine learning, with a focus on learning theory for structure-preserving kernel methods; scientific computing and data-driven learning of dynamical systems; stochastic dynamical systems and operator-theoretic methods; stochastic PDEs, Onsager–Machlup action functionals, large deviations, most probable transition pathways, and slow manifold reduction.
